Privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

This policy explains what numberforagents.com collects, why, and how long we keep it. We collect as little as we can. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use advertising trackers.

Waitlist

When you join the waitlist, we store your email address, the form or source you used, an optional note, and the signup time. We use this only to send you product updates and invites. To have your record deleted, email hello@numberforagents.com.

Page view analytics

We count page views with our own first-party script. It sets no cookies and uses no browser storage. It does not run when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. For each view we store the page path, the referring site name, campaign tags in the URL, your country, your network (ASN), and your browser user agent. To count unique visitors per day, we store a hash of your IP address and user agent with a random salt that changes every day. We delete each salt after about one day, so old hashes cannot be linked back to an IP address. We delete analytics rows after 90 days.

For automated clients such as search crawlers and AI agents, we store the same fields from the request on the server, without the visitor hash.

We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless, to see aggregate traffic.

Hosting and email

The site runs on Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes requests, including IP addresses, to serve the site and block abuse. Email to our domain is forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing to our inbox.

Your choices

  • Turn on Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control to skip our page view script.
  • Email us to see, correct, or delete your waitlist record.

Changes

We will update this page when our data practices change. Questions: hello@numberforagents.com.
